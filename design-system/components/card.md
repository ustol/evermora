# Card

Source: `src/components/ui/card.tsx`

## Purpose

Cards are the main surface for memorial summaries, KPI modules, review queues, contribution states, and dashboard panels.

## Visual rules

- Use `bg-card`, `text-card-foreground`, and `border-border/70`.
- Prefer hairline borders and warm paper fills over heavy shadows.
- Use `rounded-2xl`/`rounded-3xl` for calm editorial/admin containers.
- Keep gold accents as small rules, badges, or focus/orientation cues.

## Dashboard usage

KPI cards should show one primary number, one explanatory label, and one semantic trend/status cue. Do not use decorative color fills for all KPIs.
